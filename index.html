Rating guide

Swipe right to close.

Mood (before and after)

0Bad. Low, apathetic, hard to move or think.
1Rather bad. Sluggish or anxious, but bearable.
2Okay. Even, no strong negative and no lift.
3Good. Some energy, interest, or calm.

Pleasure

Rate the activity itself, not the relief of finishing it.

0Nothing, or unpleasant.
1Slightly pleasant, could have skipped it.
2Pleasant, wanted to keep going.
3Very. Fully absorbed in it.

Importance

Question: how much did I need this myself, regardless of whether I liked it.

0Wasted, no point.
1Just ticked it off.
2A needed thing.
3An important step, a sense of achievement.

Layers

Routine. Daily upkeep of body and home: food, shower, sleep, cleaning, medication.
Obligations. There is an external deadline or someone you owe: work, bills, paperwork, doctor.
Enjoyable. Done for pleasure or meaning. Not a reward for "after everything".

Rules

Rate right after the activity, using the first number that comes to mind.
If torn between two numbers, pick the lower one.
Zeros are expected in depression. Trends over weeks matter, not a single day.
Mood shift equals "after" minus "before".
At least one Enjoyable activity every day.
